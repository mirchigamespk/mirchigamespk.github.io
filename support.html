How do I play?

  • Drag left and right to steer.
  • Drive into passengers waving at the roadside to pick them up. Every rider pays a fare every second.
  • Carry 15 or more riders and the front wheel lifts into a wheelie: faster, with bigger fares.
  • A big load burns fuel fast, so grab the fuel cans on the road, and dodge the barriers.
  • On jumps, swipe up or down to balance in the air and land cleanly for a bonus.

Where is my progress saved?

Your rupees, chingchis, upgrades, paints and best score are saved on your phone. The game has no account, so progress doesn't move between devices, and deleting the game (or clearing its data) erases it.

The Revive or x2 Cash button says "LOADING AD..."

These buttons play a short, optional video ad. When no ad is available right now, for example without an internet connection, the button keeps trying. Check your connection, or try again on your next run.

Ads and tracking

  • iPhone: the game asks before it uses your advertising identifier for personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your ad consent on first launch. Clearing the game's data or reinstalling it shows the consent form again (this also resets your progress), or email us for help.
