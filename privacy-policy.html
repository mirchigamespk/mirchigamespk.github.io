Privacy Policy

Chacha Chingchi, by Mirchi Games. Effective date: 26 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Chacha Chingchi (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers the iPhone and Android apps and the web versions of the Game. The Game does not ask you to create an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (your rupees, chingchis, upgrades and paints), your best score and your sound on/off setting on your own device so they are there next time you play. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Information collected by third-party services (iPhone and Android apps)

The iPhone and Android versions of the Game use the following Google services. They may collect information automatically when you play:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads, including optional rewarded ads. It may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; see section 3), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalised. How Google uses information in ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent): asks for your consent where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) and stores your choice. On iPhone it also shows a short message explaining Apple's tracking request before it appears.
  • Firebase Analytics: tells us how the Game is played (for example run length, score and when a feature is used) using an app instance identifier and device information. We use it to improve gameplay and balance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: sends crash reports (device model, operating system version, app state at the time of the crash) so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config: lets us adjust game settings, such as difficulty, without an update.

These services are operated by Google LLC and process information according to the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Information may be processed on servers outside your country.

3. Tracking on iPhone

On iPhone, the Game uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. Before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, for example to show you personalised ads, the Game asks for your permission.

  • If you tap Allow, AdMob may use the advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads and to measure them.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared with us or with AdMob. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Web versions

Versions of the Game played in a web browser on game portals do not include AdMob or Firebase. Any advertising or statistics on those sites are handled by the portal itself under its own privacy policy.

5. How we use information

  • To show ads that keep the Game free.
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve it.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices

  • Where the consent form is shown, you can give, refuse or manage your ad consent. Clearing the Game's data or reinstalling it shows the form again (this also resets your progress), or you can contact us for help.
  • On iPhone, you can allow or stop tracking for the Game in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can delete the information stored on your device by deleting the Game, or on Android by clearing its data.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and security

Information collected by the services above is kept for as long as those services need it for the purposes described, in line with their own retention settings. We rely on Google's security measures for that data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
